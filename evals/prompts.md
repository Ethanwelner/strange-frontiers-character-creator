# Sample prompts

> **DRAFTS for Ethan to edit.** Claude wrote these to cover every Dreft Kind and both Tatot forms. Rewrite them in your players' real voice, add your own, and delete any that don't sound like your table.

`npm run eval` sends each prompt below to Claude and checks the build it comes back with. Each prompt is a `##` heading followed by the player's description. An optional `Expect:` line names the Kind (and form) the description points to, so the eval can flag a mismatch; leave it out when any Kind would do.

The eval reports legality on its own. Whether a character is on theme is your call: read the summaries and reasons it prints.

## Isek guild hunter

I want to play an elf who's been hunting monsters in the King's Woods for longer than most humans have been alive, and still treats it like a game. Good with a bow, bad at taking orders.

Expect: isek

## Isek runaway noble

My character is a young Isek from a noble house who ran off to see the systems and came back with a head full of foreign ideas. Smooth talker, a little reckless, owes people money.

Expect: isek

## Guwain diver

A Brine Dwarf from Glintl who dives wrecks for salvage. Heavy, slow on land, never in a hurry. Knows every captain on the western coast and most of the smugglers too.

Expect: guwain

## Guwain mountain guard

She's one of the mountain Guwain from the Corsine villages, tough as the rock, and she joined the royal guard because somebody has to keep the pass open while the nobles fight over the crown.

Expect: guwain

## Orcoid caravan boss

Big orcoid Tatot who drives caravans between the switch port and the rest of Dreft. Haggles hard, hits harder, and has buried more guards than he'd like to admit.

Expect: tatot orcoid

## Orcoid gate hand

An orcoid who works the switch port for Tetra Frontiers Co. Spends all day hauling crates and keeping foreigners out of trouble. Picked up English from the travelers and likes to show it off.

Expect: tatot orcoid

## Goblinoid licensed mage

A goblin girl from a merchant dynasty in Port Herminst who got into the licensed mage school on her family's money and is determined to prove she'd have made it anyway.

Expect: tatot goblinoid

## Goblinoid hedge witch

Small goblinoid from a farming village by the King's Woods. Never had the money for the mage schools, so she learned magic from her grandmother: herbs, charms and things the licensed mages say are dangerous.

Expect: tatot goblinoid

## Human returned from the systems

Someone from Earth who ended up in Dreft through the switch port and decided to stay. Knows a lot about technology nobody here understands and almost nothing about how things work locally.

Expect: human

## Open concept

I want to be a refugee from the Sunrise Princedoms who's trying to make a new life in Dreft. Quiet, watchful, good at not being noticed. You pick what they are.
